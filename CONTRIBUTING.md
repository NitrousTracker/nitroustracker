# NitrousTracker contribution policy

## Licensing

The project as a whole is licensed under various terms. By contributing to a given NitrousTracker organization
repository, you consent to providing it to us under the terms of that repository's license. In the typical case,
this license should be visible as a header at the top of the source files being edited.

## AI tools

Generative AI, including large language models (LLMs), should not be used in any way when contributing to
NitrousTracker.

This is a hobby project for a portable console which has been *left behind* over a decade ago. The development
team wishes to maintain a safe space to hone and develop their collaboration, triage, and programming skills
at their own, human pace.

For the sake of complete disclosure, numerous tools outside of our control, such as the GNU compiler, *do* take
advantage of generative AI tools in their development processes.

If you feel a need to use machine translation to communicate with us, we highly recommend writing in a language
you are native and/or fluent in. There may be a speaker of it among our community, and even absent one, being
aware that a translation may be flawed or not get across cultural subtleties helps foster healthy communication.

## Coding style

The project's main repositories provide a `make format` command which uses clang-format to perform automatic
formatting of the repository's code. We recommend using this tool as part of your contribution.
